Our Story

The Soul Behind the Brew

What started as a dream in a tiny kitchen has grown into a community — one cup at a time.

How it began

Where Comfort Meets Cappuccino

What started as a shared love for great coffee and warm spaces became something Guwahati didn't have yet: a café that felt like a second home. Not pretentious. Not rushed. Just genuine.

The first week, we served 23 people. By the end of the first month, we had regulars who'd come every single day.

Today, Froth & Frolic is more than a café — it's a community hub, a creative workspace, a meeting ground, and a place where the barista knows your name and your order.

What we stand for

Our Values

These aren't just words on a wall. They're the compass that guides every decision we make.

🌱 Sustainability First

From compostable cups to zero-waste kitchen practices, we're committed to treading lightly on this planet.

🤝 Community Over Competition

We collaborate with local artists, musicians, and bakers. When the community thrives, we all thrive.

✨ Uncompromising Quality

We taste-test every new recipe at least 50 times before it hits the menu. Mediocrity is not on the menu.

☕ Accessible Excellence

Great coffee shouldn't be exclusive. We keep our prices fair and our doors open to everyone.

Common questions

Before You Ask

Do you take reservations?

We're a walk-in café — no reservations needed! For private events or large groups (10+), please email us at events@frothandfrolic.com.

Do you have vegan and gluten-free options?

Absolutely! We have oat milk, almond milk, and coconut milk for all drinks. Our gluten-free banana bread and vegan brownies are legendary.

Are you open on Fridays?

We're closed on Fridays to rest, recharge, and prepare for the weekend. But we're open Mon–Thu and Sat–Sun, 10 AM – 9 PM!

Is WiFi available?

Free high-speed WiFi with every order. Just ask for the password at the counter. Power outlets at every seat — we've got you covered.
